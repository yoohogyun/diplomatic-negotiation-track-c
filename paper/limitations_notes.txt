English
Utility obtained with a fixed utterance set does not by itself establish an advantage over fixed language templates or reliable negotiation in real diplomacy. In the matched style-0 evaluation, the interpreted meanings of all 44 Luna utterances equal the canonical template meanings. This prevents attributing the measured utility advantage to language generation alone.

Other material for Methods / Evaluation / Limitations
- The exact planner has access to the declared synthetic response model and fixed interpretation mapping; it never receives the actual hidden type. Model knowledge is an additional resource, not an equal-resource comparison with model-free learning.
- Matched cases come from four documents in only two evaluation event groups. Synthetic numerical conditions and enumerated paths are not independent historical negotiations.
- The cost comparison concerns strategy selection and excludes construction/interpretation of the utterance set, teacher computation, and offline training. Zero further model calls for action selection is not zero total system cost.
- Learned-controller matched utility is 0.475 versus Sol medium 0.514 and heuristic 0.470. Keep these numbers in Results; do not imply the learned controller exceeds Sol.
- Utilities, types and opponent responses are synthetic, while source documents are real. This distinction remains in the Introduction's setup paragraph.
