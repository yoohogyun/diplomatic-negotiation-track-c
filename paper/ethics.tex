\section*{Ethical Considerations}
Public diplomatic statements provide context, not evidence of private preferences or predictions of state behavior. Synthetic payoffs and escalation indices should not guide real diplomatic decisions. Source identifiers and transformations are preserved, and access to the supplied corpus remains restricted to the research collaboration. Language models assisted utterance generation, interpretation, manuscript drafting, and a visual concept; the final teaser was redrawn as editable shapes using recorded results. Model interpretation checks are not human annotation or evidence of independent expert agreement.
